What We Offer

Self-Care Boarding, Done Right.

A complete equestrian facility built to support hands-on horse owners in Allen, Texas.

Self-Care Boarding

Enjoy the flexibility to care for your horse your way. Safe, spacious stalls with daily turnout options give your horse room to roam, while owners handle feeding, grooming, and care with full access to our well-maintained facilities.

  • Large 12×12 Stalls With Sliding Doors
  • Daily Turnout Options
  • Owner-Led Feeding & Grooming
  • Full Facility Access

Pasture Care

Large, secure pastures where your horse can graze, exercise, and relax. Fresh water, quality hay, and regular pasture maintenance ensure a safe and healthy environment.

  • 4 Spacious Pastures
  • Fresh Water & Quality Hay
  • Regular Pasture Maintenance
  • Safe Perimeter Fencing

Trailer Parking

Secure, on-site trailer parking is available for an additional fee. Designated spaces keep your trailer ready for shows, trail rides, or emergencies without off-site storage hassle.

  • Designated Parking Spaces
  • On-Site & Gated
  • Easy Access In & Out
  • Available For An Additional Fee
