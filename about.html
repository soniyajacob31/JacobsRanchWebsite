Our Story

Built Around the Bond Between the
Horse and Owner.

Where owners stay hands-on and horses come first.

A Texas Ranch Where Horses Thrive and Owners Belong.

With a passion for horses and a commitment to exceptional care, Jacob's Ranch & Stables offers a warm and welcoming environment for both horse owners and their equine companions. Nestled on 8.2 acres in Allen, Texas, we strive to make the most of everything the great outdoors has to offer.

Our self-care boarding gives you the freedom to feed, groom, exercise, and manage your horse on your own schedule, while we maintain a clean, secure, well-equipped facility that supports the bond you share.

As a family, we make sure every horse receives individualized attention, and every owner feels welcomed into a close-knit equestrian community.
